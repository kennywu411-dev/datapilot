### 40. 2025 年哪个月的退款金额占当月销售额的比例最高？给出月份（YYYY-MM）。

- 难度：challenging · 考点：子查询比率
- 口径（evidence）：有效订单指 status 不是 cancelled 和 pending 的订单；销售额指有效订单的实付金额 amount 之和；退款金额指售后工单的 refund_amount 之和；两者都按订单的下单时间归属月份
- 标准答案：`[('2025-09',)]`

```sql
SELECT m FROM (SELECT substr(o.created_at, 1, 7) AS m, SUM(o.amount) AS sales, (SELECT SUM(t.refund_amount) FROM tickets t JOIN orders o2 ON t.order_id = o2.order_id WHERE substr(o2.created_at, 1, 7) = substr(o.created_at, 1, 7)) AS refund FROM orders o WHERE o.created_at LIKE '2025%' AND o.status NOT IN ('cancelled', 'pending') GROUP BY 1) ORDER BY refund / sales DESC LIMIT 1
```
